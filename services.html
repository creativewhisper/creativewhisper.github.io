Frequently asked questions

Before
you write to us.

How do you charge?

Per project or with a monthly plan. We quote each project according to its scope and complexity. After the meeting we send you a proposal with scope, timeline and price.

Can I hire a single service?

Yes. Each service can be hired on its own or combined in a monthly plan. Combining areas almost always pays off: for example, photo and video from the same shoot, or a campaign with its WhatsApp assistant.

How long does it take?

It depends on the project. A package of social media pieces takes days; a brand video, a website or a short film takes weeks.

Do you work outside Quito?

Yes. We travel to other cities and locations; travel and logistics are added to the proposal. Data, digital marketing and automation services are delivered remotely.

Does the assistant replace my team?

No. It handles the repetitive work on WhatsApp, Instagram or your website, and passes the cases that need a person to your team. It is hired as a monthly plan, with maintenance included.

How do we start?

Message us on WhatsApp with what you have in mind. We schedule a half-hour meeting and send you the proposal.
